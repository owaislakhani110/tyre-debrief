How it works

  1. Your CSV is parsed and cleaned: sensor dropouts, spikes and off-tread zones are removed.
  2. Per-tyre statistics are computed on straights, in corners and per lap.
  3. A rule engine compares them with Team Plus guidance (60–90 °C window, camber and pressure patterns).
  4. The AI explains only those verified findings in plain English, with one priority action.
